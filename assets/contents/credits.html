The teaching method

The method rests on Total Physical Response (TPR): pairing a new word with a movement or an image trains fast recall instead of slow translation. It splits the work between two roles — the left hemisphere stores and predicts words, the right hemisphere produces them fluently in real time — and trains the second by attaching every new word to something physical before it hardens into a rule.

Every English verb falls into one of two families. V1 — nicknamed "the Godfather" — is the verb to be: strong, needs no helper, and is only ever followed by a noun. V2 covers every action verb: "weak" on its own, it always needs a helper (Did / Do / Does / Will) to ask a question, and turns into a noun-like form with -ing or -ed the moment it sits next to V1. Together they feed the Rule of Seven — Subject + Verb + Noun — presented as the formula behind roughly 95% of English answers.

On top of the grammar sits the personal dictionary technique: writing down new words, tagging their part of speech, building original sentences with them and practising collocations until a word moves from passive vocabulary — words you recognise — to active vocabulary — words you actually use.

The valley

All nine locations are real places in Simi Valley, California: the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library, Corriganville Park, Strathearn Historical Park, the Santa Susana Depot, the Chumash Indian Museum, Rocky Peak Park, the Mount McCoy cross, the Simi Valley Cultural Arts Center and Rancho Simi Community Park.

The nine objects, the Valley Archivist and the hunt itself are fiction, written for this game and inspired by the history, landscape and film heritage of the valley.

The build

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no external assets. Progress lives in your browser's local storage under a single key, so the hunt remembers you between sessions and forgets you the moment you press Reset Hunt.

Pronunciation rounds use the browser's own speech features when they are available, and fall back to a listening round when they are not.

Nine locations. Nine objects. Any order.
