Who and what made this hunt

The teaching method

The nine concepts come from the Alternative Grammar system — the author's own V1 / V2 / SVN teaching material, written for adults who need working English rather than grammar labels. The theory panels rewrite that material for a business and marketing audience; the structure, the vocabulary of the method and the mnemonics behind it belong to the author.

The valley

All nine locations are real places in Simi Valley, California: the Ronald Reagan Presidential Library, Corriganville Park, Strathearn Historical Park, the Santa Susana Depot, the Chumash Indian Museum, Rocky Peak Park, the Mount McCoy cross, the Simi Valley Cultural Arts Center and Rancho Simi Community Park.

The nine objects, the Valley Archivist and the hunt itself are fiction, written for this game and inspired by the history, landscape and film heritage of the valley.

The build

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No frameworks, no build step, no external assets. Progress lives in your browser's local storage under a single key, so the hunt remembers you between sessions and forgets you the moment you press Reset Hunt.

Pronunciation rounds use the browser's own speech features when they are available, and fall back to a listening round when they are not.

Nine locations. Nine objects. Any order.
