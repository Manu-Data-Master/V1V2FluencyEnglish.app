The Rule of Seven

One shape covers almost every answer you will give

The oldest house in this park was built from a plan so simple the family could raise the frame themselves. English answers work the same way. There is one frame, and roughly nineteen out of twenty sentences sit on it.

S + V + N .

Subject — who or what. Verb — V1 or V2. Noun — the thing, the feeling or the situation. Then stop. The full stop is part of the rule: adults lose sentences by continuing past the noun.

The habit to break

Spanish lets you drop the subject. English does not. "Is ready" is not a sentence; "The demo is ready" is. When a sentence collapses mid-air, the missing block is almost always the subject.

How to use it live

Before you speak, decide the three blocks in order: who, which verb, what thing. Say them. Stop. Anything extra — time, place, detail — you can add afterwards as a second short sentence.
