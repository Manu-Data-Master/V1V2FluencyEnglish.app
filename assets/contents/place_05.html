Weak verbs, and the three helpers that carry them

Every basket in this valley was built by repeating one weave until the shape held. V2 questions work the same way: one pattern, three helpers, endless sentences.

Helper + S + V2 + N ?

Choose the helper, keep the verb plain
TimeHelperQuestionAnswer
PastDid — every subjectDid the client approve the budget?The client approved the budget.
PresentDo (I, you, we, they) · Does (he, she, it)Does she manage the account?She manages the account.
FutureWill — every subjectWill they sign this week?They will sign this week.

The rule that saves you every time

The helper carries the tense, so the verb stays plain. Did he run — not "did he ran". Does she manage — not "does she manages". The past or the "-s" appears only when the helper disappears.

Answers: the long way and the natural way

  • Did he present the results? → Long: He did present the results. Natural: He presented the results.
  • Does he report to you? → Long: He does report to me. Natural: He reports to me.

Use the long form when you want emphasis — it is how English insists.

Negatives: helper + not, always before the verb

They did not (didn't) send the proposal. We do not (don't) discount below ten percent. She will not (won't) join the call.
