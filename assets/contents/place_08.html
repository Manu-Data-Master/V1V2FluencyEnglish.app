The Sound of American English

Hidden here: Echo Stage Mask 🎭 — clear ten rounds and it is yours.

Two sounds carry most of the accent

On this stage, actors are trained to find a sound in the body before they find it in the script. American English has two sounds that Spanish speakers rarely make by accident, and both have a physical shortcut.

The angry dog 🐕

The American R — a low growl, tongue pulled back, jaw loose. It never taps like the Spanish r.

Practise on: person, work, first, return, quarter.

The happy dog 🐶

The long E — a wide smile, held about three seconds when you drill it. In a southern accent it stretches even further.

Practise on: meeting, team, key, agree, stream.

The third fix: -tion is "shun"

presentation, information, production, negotiation. Say the ending like the English word "shun" and one whole family of vocabulary lands correctly.
